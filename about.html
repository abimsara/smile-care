About SmileCare

A family-run dental practice in Colombo, built on one simple idea: good dentistry should never feel intimidating.

Our Story

Caring for Colombo's smiles since 2007

SmileCare opened its doors in 2007 with two chairs, one dentist and a waiting room that was far too small. What we lacked in space we made up for in time — time to listen, time to explain, and time to make sure nobody left anxious.

Eighteen years later we've grown into a four-specialist clinic with digital X-rays, same-day crowns and a paediatric room that kids genuinely ask to visit. But the principle hasn't changed: you are a person in our chair, not a chart number.

Whether you need a simple cleaning or a complex implant, we'll tell you exactly what's happening, what it costs, and what your options are — in plain language, with no pressure.

Our Values

What we stand for

Four principles that shape every decision we make in the clinic.

Compassion

We treat anxiety as a real thing, not an inconvenience. Sedation, breaks and reassurance are always available.

Safety First

Hospital-grade sterilisation, single-use disposables and strict cross-infection protocols on every visit.

Honesty

Written estimates before treatment. If you don't need it, we'll tell you — even if it means less revenue for us.

Affordability

0% instalment plans, insurance support and transparent pricing so cost is never the reason you wait.
